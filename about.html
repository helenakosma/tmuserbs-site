Who we are

The Serbian Student Association brings together TMU students who share an interest in Serbian culture, language, food and music.

We host socials, cultural nights and celebrations for holidays like Božić, Slava and Vidovdan. We team up with other cultural clubs at TMU and around Toronto. You don't need to be Serbian to join. Everyone is welcome.
